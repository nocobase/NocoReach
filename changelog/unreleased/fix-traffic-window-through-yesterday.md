## Traffic: date ranges reach yesterday

- Traffic's date ranges end yesterday in the GA4 property's time zone instead of at the synced cutoff three days back, so Last 7 days is the last seven full days. The last two days count in the figures and are dotted on the chart and in a row's detail, because GA4 is still processing them; the page notes mention them only when the range reaches them.
- Traffic's report is read again at midnight in the property's time zone, when its range moves, and once more in the morning after a visit, as GA4 is still filling in yesterday just after midnight. A project nobody opens has it read once a day. A row's detail is kept for long only once GA4 has processed its days.
- A page's search queries on Traffic still come from Search Console's finalized data, which ends a few days earlier. The page notes, the page's detail and its hand-off brief now give those dates.
- The chat panel's data tool tells the agent which Traffic days GA4 is still processing and which days a page's search queries cover.
