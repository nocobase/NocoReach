## Search results: Google's hidden result links

- Google now hides many organic result links behind `google.com/goto` redirects. Every organic Google result is read through one helper, which credits it to the site Google shows under the title, never to google.com, and keeps the redirect as its link.
- Rank tracking finds our site from that line and its page by its title, and checks through DataForSEO's Standard queue, which resolves the hidden links and costs $0.006 a check of the top 100 instead of $0.02. A check where most links stayed hidden and our site was not found is marked Uncertain and never counts as a drop; checks saved since late October whose results were mostly hidden are marked the same way once.
- Keyword details' Top 10 shows each result's real site, and Rank history marks uncertain checks.
- Reddit searches also use the Standard queue (about $0.03 a term a week). A hidden result counts only when it matches a known thread by subreddit and title; the page says how many were skipped.
