## Chat panel: long answers are no longer cut off at four minutes, a cut-off answer continues, and errors say what happened

- A reply in the chat panel no longer stops after a fixed four minutes. It stops only when nothing has come for 90 seconds (no text, reasoning, tool call or tool result) or after 15 minutes in all. A long analytical answer through a slow relay (one real reply: 30,080 input and 5,844 output tokens with several tables and `query_data` rounds) used to time out every time, and sending it again regenerated the same answer and timed out again.
- A reply that stopped partway through no fault of the question (stalled, the 15-minute limit, NocoReach restarted, the model service or NocoReach unreachable, rate limited, ended early, or the model's own output limit) keeps what it wrote and shows why in one quiet line with Continue, on the conversation's latest turn, in the chat panel and in the Insights day conversation. Continue queues the same turn again: its unfinished last line and tool calls still waiting for a result are dropped, the model is given the rest, with the tool results it already read, and asked to carry on from there, so the reply reads as one answer and no data is read twice. A reply that had written nothing offers Try again instead. Other failures keep the red alert and are sent again by hand.
- Errors say what happened: NocoReach restarting, NocoReach or the model service unreachable, stalled, the 15-minute limit, model quota, credentials or rate limit, the model refusing the request, model configuration, and an unknown cause, which no longer says to check the model's configuration (`modelFailed` became `unknown`). Requests from the panel that get a gateway's 502-504 or no answer at all say NocoReach cannot be reached right now and may be updating.
- A gateway's bare 502-504 page anywhere in the app now reads "NocoReach did not respond just now (the server may be updating). Try again in a moment." instead of "Request did not complete (502)". The proposal cards in the chat (Keywords' lookup, Analysis rules' change) read the list or rules again after such a failure, so a lookup or change that went through shows as running or saved, and Confirm stays to try again otherwise.
- A worker renews its claim on a reply every 15 seconds while it runs (60-second lease, until now a fixed 270 seconds from the claim), so after a restart a reply nobody is writing reads as interrupted within a minute, and a long reply another worker is still writing is never taken for dead. Errors while NocoReach stops are recorded as interrupted.
- `POST /onlineAgents/runs/:id/continue` (owner only, the conversation's latest turn). Copy in all twelve UI languages (`onlineAgent.stalled`, `serviceUnavailable`, `modelRejected`, `unknown`, `notContinuable`, `offline`, `continue`, `continuedTimes`, `common.serviceUnavailable`, and rewritten `timeout`, `interrupted`, `modelNetwork`, `modelRateLimit`, `incompleteResponse`, `length`, `requestFailed`).
