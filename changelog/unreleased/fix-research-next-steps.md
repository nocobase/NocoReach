- Keyword research now continues with Write blog post first, then New docs page and Improve existing page. Content topic and outline lists Write blog post first, and Competitor comparison adds it after New docs page.

- A playbook is never shown by its id: while the project's playbooks are loading, the review card's button and next-step choice and the start card's playbook name built-ins by their own names, and leave out any other until the list arrives.
