## Tasks: the Snooze dialog says what snoozing does

- The Snooze dialog explains what it is for (the task waits on something outside NocoReach), where the task is meanwhile (Tasks › Snoozed, its progress kept) and what happens on the day (it goes back to where it was and its owner is reminded). It no longer mentions Waiting for me.
- The field reads only "Snooze until"; the time zone moves into the sentence below it, such as "Back on Thu, Dec 10 at 9 AM (China Standard Time), and Super Admin is reminded". Chinese shows mainland China's time zone as Beijing time instead of GMT+8.
