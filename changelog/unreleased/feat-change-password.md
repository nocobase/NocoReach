## Passwords: change your own, and administrators reset a member's

- Account menu › Change password asks for the current password and the new one twice. Other browsers and command-line sign-ins are signed out; the current page stays signed in.
- Members & roles › a member's row menu › Reset password offers a randomly generated password that can be copied or replaced, and signs the member out everywhere. It refuses your own account, system administrators and members holding more than you may grant.
