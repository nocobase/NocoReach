## Keywords › AI suggestions: only keywords people search

- AI suggestions starts from demand, not from the product's own wording (2026-10-27: many keywords the AI invented had no searches). The AI chooses 8–15 short seed words the way people search, in the market's language, from project memory, the site's Search Console queries and the competitors; DataForSEO returns the real keywords for them (keyword suggestions per seed, keyword ideas for all seeds together, and the volume of the site's Search Console queries); only keywords with at least 50 monthly searches in the market are kept (`MIN_VOLUME`); the AI picks about 40 of those, never adding its own, with the group, a one-line reason and the site page that could answer each (or No suitable page). The list is ranked by opportunity: search volume, relevance, difficulty and the site's position. A keyword without volume never appears.
- Every lookup is a run of the shared DataForSEO layer: inside the monthly budget, a seed looked up in the last 30 days is read back for free, and an uncertain result is never retried automatically (the seed is listed as returning nothing this time). A run costs at most $0.25 (12 seeds: about $0.20; the same seeds again: nothing); the page says so before the first run, Edit seed words shows the most the next run can cost and this month's spending before Find again, and a run that would pass the budget is refused before anything is asked. Runs go on in the background, one per project, and the page shows the stage.
- The line above the list says how it was made: the seed words (shown as chips), how many real keywords were found, how many were picked, the volume floor, the market and the date, with the cost in its ⓘ. Edit seed words (also the refresh button) edits the seeds and the market, or lets the AI choose them again, before running.
- Digging further goes through the chat panel, with no input on the page: Ask AI to dig for keywords opens a conversation that has read the market, the seeds and the list, with four prompts; Dig from this keyword, shown on a row's hover, opens it on that keyword. The chat's `keyword_dig` tool runs the same pipeline from seeds or a direction, states the most it may cost and runs only after the user agrees, then adds the result as a group tab named after the direction ("From approval workflow 18") without the keywords the list already has, or rebuilds the list for another market. The conversation shows a card linking to the group, and the page updates at once.
- Saving from AI suggestions keeps the market numbers the keyword was picked with. Find keywords, Saved and the keyword page are unchanged. A list made before this change is not shown; the page offers to make a new one.
- `DataTable` takes an optional `rowClassName` (used here for the row's `group`); the seventeen other pages that use it are unchanged. The demo answers the new seed and pick steps and the chat's keyword prompts; the simulated integrations replace `keyword-suggest` with `keyword-seeds` and `keyword-curate`.
