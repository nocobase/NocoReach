## Opportunities: a Skill's finding no longer splits one opportunity in two

- The daily analysis recognizes an opportunity it raised before by the changes the server computes, not by its Skills' findings, whose keys change daily with the keywords cited. An opportunity resting on Skill findings alone matches one with a finding of the same Skill and kind about the same page or under the same title apart from its numbers. Ignored opportunities stay ignored by the same rule.
- When the AI names the opportunity it raised before, an objective that differs only in its numbers no longer makes it a new one.
- Opportunities already split read as one: the earlier copy reads as Expired, raised again by a later analysis, and only the later one stays pending.
