## Traffic: the tick reads only what the page shows first; the other tables are read when the page is opened

- Traffic's GA4 reports come in two parts. The figures, the line, Channel, New and returning, Device, Source / medium, Events and the data quality notes take seven reports (Channel and the channels behind a change are now one), which the sync tick reads ahead as before. Campaign, Landing pages, Pages, Geography, Browser and OS, and the pages behind a change, take ten, and are read when the page is opened, right after its figures show, before any tab is clicked. For a project opened in the last day the sync tick also reads them once a day, once GA4 has most of yesterday, so their tabs and the online agent answer at once. A project nobody opens costs seven GA4 reports a window instead of 18, and one in daily use 24 a day instead of 36.
- A tab whose table is still being read shows it loading; a failure to read those tables shows on their tabs only, and the figures above stay. While those tables are a copy of the previous day's, the page says so, and the change sentence names pages only once they cover the same days as the figures.
- The online agent reads those tables only when asked for one of them.
