## Traffic: the tick reads only what the page shows first; the other tables are read when the page is opened

- Traffic's GA4 reports come in two parts. The figures, the line, Channel, New and returning, Device, Source / medium, Events and the data quality notes take eight reports, which the sync tick reads ahead as before. Campaign, Landing pages, Pages, Geography, Browser and OS, and the pages behind a change, take ten, and are read when the page is opened, right after its figures show, before any tab is clicked. A project nobody opens costs eight GA4 reports a window instead of 18.
- A tab whose table is still being read shows it loading; a failure to read those tables shows on their tabs only, and the figures above stay. While those tables are a copy of the previous day's, the page says so, and the change sentence names pages only once they cover the same days as the figures.
- The online agent reads those tables only when asked for one of them.
