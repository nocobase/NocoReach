## Articles: the primary language follows the project, and can be chosen at hand-over and changed before approval

- With no language chosen, an article task writes the project's AI content language first when its target has it, then the target's other languages; otherwise the target's order as before. The start card, Accept and start, and a translation's default source follow the same rule.
- Continue with offers Primary language when the next playbook writes an article, and the new task starts with it.
- Until the primary language is approved, the article review card's Primary language can change it: the Agent writes a new version in it, Activity records the change, and the other languages, the former primary included, are translated after approval.
- The Agent is told that the primary language is changed on the review card, and never to point to a setting the task page lacks.
