## Search performance and Traffic: open without waiting for Google

- Search performance builds its query and page tables from the data already synced when it holds every day of both periods, so opening the page asks Search Console nothing. A filter, a country, a recent project or a range longer than three months still reads Search Console.
- A report past its lifetime answers at once from its saved copy while a new one is read in the background, and the page replaces it by itself. A new day's date range answers the same way from the previous day's, except Country / region, Device, Search appearance and a row's detail, which load on their own. If a new one cannot be read, a line under the date range (and in Sitemaps and a row's detail) says when the report shown was saved and why, such as an authorization that has expired.
- Reports over final days are kept for 90 days instead of six hours, so opening a row or a breakdown of the same days again does not ask Google. A fixed range ending on the latest final day is read once more the next day, in case Google published that day late.
- Exports, hand-offs and a row's detail use the period the tables hold, and the online agent's tables always cover the period it states.
- The scheduled sync readies the default range's tables and breakdowns too, so they are there before anyone opens the page, for projects someone has opened in the last three days or just connected. Other projects cost no Google requests for pages nobody looks at.
- Until the day's sync lands, Search performance's tables end at the last synced day instead of asking Search Console, and the ⓘ names the period they cover.
- Traffic's report is read at most twice a day instead of every six hours, so the preliminary days at the end of its line can lag by up to a day.
