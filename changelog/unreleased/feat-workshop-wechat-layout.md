## Content workshop: WeChat layout

- What to make ends with WeChat layout for an article. Preview and copy shows the article's body as written, laid out for the WeChat Official Account editor at a phone's width, with Copy title and Copy body; no task is created. The body is read from the publishing target holding the article; when it cannot be read, the row says so and the button is disabled. A new topic has no such row.
- Every element is styled inline, as the editor keeps no stylesheets. Links to other sites, which WeChat articles cannot open, become numbered references listed at the end; links to other WeChat articles stay. Code blocks keep their lines and indentation, images stay, and the title check warns when the title is over WeChat's 64-byte limit.
- Brand and templates adds WeChat layout: the body font size (15 px by default) and line height (1.85 by default). Headings, bold text and reference numbers use the brand color.
- The demo's blog CMS holds two Library articles with bodies to lay out.
