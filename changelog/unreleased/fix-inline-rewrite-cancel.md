## Tasks: Rewrite with AI and the link box can always be cancelled and no longer cover the article

- The box that asks how to rewrite a selection has a Cancel button (Esc), and a click anywhere outside it closes it, the selection kept. Once a rewrite is being written or shown, a click outside leaves it open, so a stray click does not lose it.
- Esc works wherever focus is on the page: it closes the box before anything is asked, stops a rewrite being written, and discards one shown. Before, only Esc in the box itself closed it.
- The bar sits in room of its own under the paragraph or the new text, so the text after it moves down instead of being covered, and the box is as wide as its hint, so the whole example shows.
- The article editor's link box (⌘K) works the same way: a Cancel button (Esc), Esc from anywhere on the page, a click outside closes it with the selection kept, and it sits in room of its own under the paragraph. The address field now takes focus when the box opens, the words being linked stay marked, and the Rewrite with AI button stays out of the way until the box closes.
