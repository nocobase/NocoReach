## Chat panel: a conversation that cannot be read again says so above the composer

- When an open conversation can no longer be read (NocoReach restarting while an answer is being written, for example), the error and its Retry button now show just above the composer, where the reader follows the latest reply. Before, the error sat at the top of the conversation, out of sight while the panel kept the latest reply in view, so the reader only saw "Replying…". The error clears on the next successful read, as before, and a conversation that has not loaded at all still shows its error in place of the conversation.
- When the error appears and the conversation area gets shorter, the latest reply stays in view.
