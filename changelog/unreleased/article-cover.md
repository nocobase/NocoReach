## Articles: covers that follow each site's settings

- A publishing target keeps its cover settings: the field covers go in (shared by every language, or one per language), the ratio, the narrowest width and whether new articles need one. They are detected from the site's latest 20 articles when the target is saved and on every Test connection, and an administrator can change them from the Cover row on the target's card (Data sources › Blog CMS); settings saved there are kept over later detections. A target without a cover field has no cover row anywhere.
- The review card, and its full-screen page, has a Cover row under Status: Add cover takes an upload, a drop, a paste or an image from the body or the task; a set cover shows as a thumbnail at the site's ratio with its size, Replace and Remove; an existing article keeps the site's cover unless it is replaced. An image of another ratio is cropped to the site's ratio first (Adjust cover), and a cover narrower than the minimum is noted as possibly blurry.
- A site that requires covers refuses approval of a new article published at once without one; a draft may go without. A cover change is saved automatically, becomes part of the next version, is noted in Activity and shows old and new thumbnails in Compare with original. The Agent never sets covers.
- Publishing writes a cover only when the article changes it: into the NocoBase attachment field's storage, into the repository's cover folder with the front matter key, or through the article API's new optional `POST /images` and `GET /capabilities` (docs/ARTICLE-API.md). Other targets get the same image; one without covers lists it as left out.
