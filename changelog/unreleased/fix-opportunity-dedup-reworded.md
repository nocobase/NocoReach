## Opportunities: a reworded opportunity keeps its number

- The daily analysis shows the model every undecided opportunity, however large the evidence, so it can name the one it raises again; before, only the latest 15 went when the input ran over budget.
- An opportunity raised again in other words keeps its number when it cites the same changes and its title shares a term with the earlier one once numbers and generic words are left out. Two opportunities one analysis raised stay two, and ignored opportunities are matched as before.
- Page targets compare whatever their scheme, `www.`, trailing slash, query or fragment.
- Opportunities already split read as one: the earlier copy reads as Expired, raised again by a later analysis.
