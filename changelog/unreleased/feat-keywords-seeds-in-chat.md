## Keywords › AI suggestions: seed words are adjusted in the chat

- The seed-word dialog (market, seed list, Let AI choose again, Find again) and the page's Find again button are gone (2026-10-28 user decision: what needs a conversation over several turns lives in the chat panel). The explanation line and the seed chips stay, read-only. Adjust seed words with AI opens the chat on the seed words with five prompts (Explain how these seed words were chosen, Choose seed words that lean toward use cases, Add or remove a seed word, Find again with the current seed words, Switch to another market); a seed chip opens it on that seed (Which keywords did this seed word find?, Replace this seed word). The market changes in the chat too, since another market means new seed words in its language and a paid lookup to confirm.
- Each seed now records why it was chosen (the inputs it came from: project memory, the site's Search Console queries, competitors, the direction asked for, or the user) and how many real keywords it found and how many were picked; the chat explains the seeds from these. Lists made before this change say the reasons were not recorded.
- The chat no longer runs lookups. `propose_keyword_run` (replacing `keyword_dig`) returns a proposal drawn in the conversation: the seed words before and after (removed ones struck through, added ones marked), why the AI chose new ones, the market, the most the lookup may cost with the seeds reused within 30 days, and this month's spending against the budget. Confirm and look up runs it through the page's own action (`suggest` for the list, the new `dig` for a group); the card follows the run, the page updates without a reload, and a proposal the list moved past reads Out of date. When the seed words come out the same as now, the card and the AI say so and why, instead of a silent refresh. This also replaces typing "confirm" for a dig, which was ambiguous.
- The most a run may cost is computed on DataForSEO Labs' current list price ($0.012 a request and $0.00012 a keyword, the seed's own row counted): at most $0.30 for 15 seeds. A real 15-seed run on Studio cost $0.2501, above the $0.25 the old price gave. The request specs keep their stored price, as it is part of each run's 30-day reuse key.
- `nrKeywordSuggest.estimate` and `nrKeywordSuggest.seeds` are removed (only the dialog used them); `nrKeywordSuggest.dig` is added under Analyze.
- Copy in all twelve UI languages (`keywordSuggestions.adjustSeeds`, `keywordSuggestions.seedEntry`, `keywordSuggestions.preset*`, group management and error copy, `keywordRun.*`, `keywordSuggest.*`, `onlineAgent.proposeKeywordRun`); the dialog's copy is removed.
- Group tabs can be managed (2026-10-28, from the Studio list where dug groups ran three lines deep). A dug group's ⋯ menu, shown on hover, renames it or deletes it; deleting removes its keywords from AI suggestions and keeps them out of later lookups, while saved keywords stay saved. The four built-in groups have no menu. Ticked rows get Remove beside Save: removed keywords leave the list and do not come back in later digs or re-finds unless the user asks for them back in the chat (`restore` on `propose_keyword_run`).
- A dug group has a short name in the user's language (`name`, about 16 characters, from the chat or a rename) with the full direction on hover; groups from before show their direction cut at a word. A dig in a direction already dug adds to that group (the card says so) instead of opening a second tab, and groups saved twice in one direction show as one.
- The tabs stay on one line: those that do not fit go under More, and the selected group is always shown.
- A failed dig ("nothing new in this direction") no longer shows above a full list: it is said in its chat card, and its message now says the list already has everything found. A failed re-find of the list shows only when it is newer than the list, saying the list below is the earlier one.
- Actions `nrKeywordSuggest.renameDig`, `deleteDig` and `removeItems` (Analyze); no edit while a lookup is running.
