## Tasks: review steps no longer ask you to tick items before approving

- The review card and the full-screen article no longer show "Confirm the following". Accept, Approve and Publish are no longer disabled until the boxes are ticked. Built-in research playbooks no longer ask you to confirm that cited data and facts are accurate: approving already means you have checked the result.
- Tasks started before this change still have the item in their saved steps. They now accept without it, and their saved steps show no item. New reviews no longer record ticked items; reviews recorded earlier keep them.
- The playbook editor no longer has "Confirm during review", and the steps list no longer shows items to confirm. Custom and edited playbooks saved with items load without them and drop them the next time playbooks are saved, and a save from an older client that still sends them ignores them. New playbook with AI no longer asks for or returns items.
- The confirmation for changes to files outside the task's directories stays: a pull request that changes other files still needs its box ticked before it is published.
