## Charts: toggle trend series from the legend

- Click a legend item to hide or restore its series across traffic, GitHub, downloads, forum, search performance and indexing, keyword details, content details, and Google Trends charts. Hidden items stay available with reduced opacity and strikethrough, and support keyboard interaction.
- Switching metrics starts each chart with every series visible, including search and download detail trends.
- Default tooltips and automatic Y axes follow the visible series; hiding every series keeps the chart available.
