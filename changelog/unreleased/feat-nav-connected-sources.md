## Menu: a data page shows once its source is connected

- Site & product, Search & market and Community list only the pages whose source is connected, as the classic portal did: Traffic and Search performance with Google (GA4 and Search Console), Pages with website content, Site audit with website content or DataForSEO, Keywords with Search Console, DataForSEO or saved keywords, Competitors with competitor watch or DataForSEO, AI search with DataForSEO, and each other page with its own source. A group with no page left disappears; Insights, Opportunities, Tasks, Results, AI team and Settings always show.
- Xiaohongshu, Bilibili and YouTube stay in the menu after a disconnect while their saved data is still on the page, until it is cleared.
- Connecting a source on Settings › Data connections says what it added, such as "Added to the menu: Community › Bilibili", with Open page.
- A hidden page's address still opens it, with its prompt to connect. The menu does not flicker: a reload draws the last menu at once, a failed refresh keeps it, and a first read that fails shows every page.
- Insights cards, analysis chips and situation lines, and links in conversations, no longer lead to a page the menu hides: they show their numbers or text without the link.
