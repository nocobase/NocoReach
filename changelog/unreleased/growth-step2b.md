## Opportunities: trending topics, questions asked often and new data sources

- Each followed topic is counted per week on Reddit, Hacker News, YouTube and GitHub new repositories, with eight weeks of history. A topic whose last 4 weeks are clearly up on the 4 weeks before on at least two sources becomes a Trending now opportunity (Topic discussed more), with Google's search change over 3 months and how many of Google's top 10 are dedicated to it. A rise on one source alone waits under Watching until another source rises as well. The opportunity and the watched change show the weeks as a Discussions a week chart.
- Once a week the AI reads the followed topics' discussions for topics people talk about more and more that are not followed yet (New topic). The opportunity says which followed topic's discussions it came from, with Add to followed topics (at most 10 topics).
- Hot discussion: a Hacker News story on a followed topic with enough points, with its comments and the products they name, and a Reddit thread of the week that Google ranks in its first three for the topic, with its position, post date and the products named in Google's text. Reddit shows no votes or comments, which it does not give through Google. The Agent drafts a reply and you post it yourself.
- Asked again and again: the AI groups the last 30 days' forum topics and the followed topics' Reddit and Hacker News posts by what they ask; a question asked in five or more posts with no page answering it is a New content opportunity.
- Popular video: a YouTube video on a followed topic published in the last 30 days with enough views is a Trending now opportunity.
- Other-language market: the AI suggests up to 5 markets for the product (changeable under the rule in Analysis rules), puts up to 200 main keywords the way people there search, and reads their searches over 12 months from Google Ads (DataForSEO Standard queue) with up to three competitors' presence there. A market searched enough where the site has no pages in its language is a New content opportunity.
- Searched on the site, no page: what people searched on the site in the last 28 days (GA4 `view_search_results`, free) and no page covers. When GA4 records no site search, Analysis rules says how to turn it on.
- People also ask: the questions on the results pages of up to 20 main keywords that no page answers, per keyword group, with the related searches.
- Sites linking to competitors: sites linking to at least two of up to three competitors and not to us, by authority. The Agent drafts the outreach email and you send it.
- Analysis rules has a rule for each, with its thresholds, and each paid rule says its monthly cost at most: Other-language markets about $0.45, People also ask $0.04, Link gap $0.08, topic results pages $0.03 (about $0.60 a project together, within the DataForSEO monthly budget). Site search, Hacker News and the weekly counts are free.
- Demo: ACME's Opportunities lead with the AI project assistant topic and hold 4 Trending now, 3 New content, 3 Competitor gaps and 2 Content optimization in To do, with a topic rising on Reddit alone under Watching; ACME CRM adds a rising topic, a Hacker News discussion, a site search with no results and People also ask. The simulated connections answer GA4 site search and Google Ads search volume.
