## Trends: related searches say what they are and which query they come from

- The info tip next to "Related searches for ..." follows Google Trends' own help: these are searches made in the same search session by the people who searched for the term; Top ranks them by relative popularity, 100 being the most searched among them; Rising lists the ones that grew most since the previous period, the percentage being that growth.
- Above the lists, a line names the query's conditions as Google Trends names its options: the region and the dates the lists were queried for, All categories and Web Search. Followed topics show the same line in their drawer. Each query now fixes its dates (the past 12 months up to that day) before asking and keeps them with its region, so after switching the region, before the next query, or after a refresh that failed, the line still describes the lists shown. Lists saved by an earlier version take their region from the link DataForSEO returned with them; when it cannot tell, the region shows as Unknown and there is no link.
- "Open in Google Trends" now opens the current Explore page with the same terms, region and dates as the saved lists, built by NocoReach, instead of the classic Explore link DataForSEO returns, whose numbers did not match. The product comparison, the product's related searches and each followed topic have their own link. A comma inside a term becomes a space in the link, as Google Trends would otherwise split it into two terms. The number and cost of DataForSEO queries do not change.
