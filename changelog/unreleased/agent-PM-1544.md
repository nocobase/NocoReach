## Trends: related searches say what they are and which query they come from

- The info tip next to "Related searches for ..." follows Google Trends' own help: these are searches made by the people who searched for the term; Top ranks them by relative popularity, 100 being the most searched among them; Rising lists the ones that grew most since the previous period, the percentage being that growth.
- Above the lists, a line names the query's conditions as Google Trends names its options: the region, Past 12 months, All categories and Web Search. Followed topics show the same line in their drawer.
- "Open in Google Trends" now opens the current Explore page with the same terms, region and time range, built by NocoReach, instead of the classic Explore link DataForSEO returns, whose numbers did not match. The product comparison, the product's related searches and each followed topic have their own link. The number and cost of DataForSEO queries do not change.
