## Playbooks, Skills and Opportunities: save feedback in the drawer

- Saving a playbook or a Skill in its drawer marks each missing field where it is and says why by the Save button; the drawer stays open with what was typed, instead of an error at the top of the page behind the overlay.
- What was saved, enabled, duplicated, restored or deleted is a toast, shown above the drawer.
- Why ignoring or restoring an opportunity or a change failed is said in its drawer.
