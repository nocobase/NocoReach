## Content workshop: Carousel for Xiaohongshu, Instagram and LinkedIn

- What to make offers Carousel in place of Xiaohongshu note, with three places, one task each: Xiaohongshu (3:4, 1080×1440, a note title and body), Instagram (4:5, 1080×1350, 6–20 cards, a caption and up to 5 hashtags) and LinkedIn (4:5, 6–18 pages in one PDF, a document title and post text). Trending now and Opportunities' Adapt into short posts use the same row. Xiaohongshu tasks made before count as Carousel › Xiaohongshu.
- Each place has its own built-in playbook and Skill (`instagram-carousel`, `linkedin-carousel`) and its own checks: card count, cover title, colors, overflow, Instagram's hashtags and links in the caption, LinkedIn's document title and post text. Cards written in English and other languages without Chinese, Japanese or Korean are counted in words: 40 a card and 8 on the cover.
- The review card shows each place's own texts, with Instagram's caption opening marked and LinkedIn's pages numbered. The done card downloads Instagram's images numbered 01–NN with Copy caption and Copy hashtags, and LinkedIn's pages as one PDF with Copy document title and Copy post text. Edit directly works for every place at its own size.
- Brand and templates previews the card style at 3:4 and 4:5. Make a new style with AI checks both sizes with full Chinese and English cards, and a saved style that does not fit at 4:5 says so and can be adjusted with AI.
- Library shows one Carousel icon for every place, the WeChat layout row shows only for Chinese articles, and the demo adds an Instagram task awaiting review and a LinkedIn task that is done.
