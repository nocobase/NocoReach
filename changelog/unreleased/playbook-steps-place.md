## Tasks: View steps says where the task changes things

- On a task, View steps (on the start card and on what the task was started with) words its change step and Publish by where the change goes: an article task edits the article in its publishing target (or writes a new one for it) and publishes to its targets; a change of the site still opens a PR and Publish merges it. Change steps of an article task show an edit icon instead of the PR icon.
- Where the place is not known yet (Playbooks, and the start card before the article is settled), the words cover both: the step opens a PR for the site repository or edits the article in the CMS, and Publish merges the PR or publishes to the CMS.
- An analysis step reads "Writes a document without changing the site" instead of naming the repository.
