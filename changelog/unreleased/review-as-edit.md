## Tasks: review an article by editing it, and rewrite a selection with AI

- An article's review card is its editor: title and summary as fields, the body as formatted text with the formatting toolbar and links, written back as Markdown with untouched blocks kept as they were. Edit directly is gone.
- Edits are saved automatically a moment after each change, without becoming a version, and come back after a refresh or on another computer. Approve and Request changes make a version of them first; Activity says how many places AI rewrote and how many were changed by hand. Discard unversioned changes returns to the version under review.
- One person edits an article at a time; anyone else reads it with the editor named until they leave.
- Compare with original shows the edited article against the one it was written from.
- Select part of the body and choose Rewrite with AI (⌘J): AI rewrites only the selection with the project's chat model, streamed under the struck-through original. Accept, discard, or ask again and go back to earlier attempts; ⌘Z undoes an accepted rewrite.
- Request changes on an article opens a box in the card for changes across the whole article, which the Agent makes from the edited version.
