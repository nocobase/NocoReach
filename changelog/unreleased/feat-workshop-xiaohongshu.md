## Content workshop: Xiaohongshu cards

- What to make offers Xiaohongshu notes: the agent turns an article or topic into 6–18 card images of 1080×1440 with the note's title and body, following the new Xiaohongshu cards playbook and its `xhs-cards` Skill. It renders the cards on its own computer from a template drawn in the brand style and uploads them with the new `task file` command; the images are kept with the result and readable by whoever can read the task.
- The review card shows the cards as a gallery, each with the source sections it comes from; a card over 110 characters, or one the agent found overflowing, is framed in yellow. NocoReach's checks cover the source sections used, the cover heading within 20 characters, the number of cards, at most 3 colors (counted from the images) and each card's length, and show the agent's overflow check when it reported one. Editing card text during review is not available yet.
- A finished set offers Download N images as one ZIP, Copy title and Copy body. Records name the number of cards.
- Brand and templates adds Brand style: a logo (the website's icon unless one is uploaded), three colors, the font and a fixed closing page, with a preview of the cover, a content card and the closing page.
- The demo's main project adds two Xiaohongshu tasks with rendered cards, one done and one waiting for review with a card over the limit.
