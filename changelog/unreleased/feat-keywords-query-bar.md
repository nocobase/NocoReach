## Keywords › AI suggestions: query bar, results summary and Selection rules

- The top of AI suggestions reads like a keyword tool (after Google Keyword Planner, Semrush and Ahrefs) instead of a sentence of AI output: one card with the seed words, each with how many of its keywords were picked (the most first, a seed with none greyed), the market, Edit seed words and one AI entry, Ask AI to find keywords, which opens the chat with four prompts (how the seeds were chosen, add or remove seeds, expand in a direction, why a keyword was not picked). The sentence, its Edit and Adjust seed words with AI links and Ask AI to dig for keywords are gone.
- A seed word's popover says where it came from, why, how many keywords it found and picked; Show only these narrows the list to its keywords, and Remove this seed word keeps the seeds without it for the next re-find, at no cost. Another market from the query bar opens Edit seed words to find the list again there.
- A results summary above the group tabs follows the selected tab: keywords, monthly searches added up (compact in the reader's language from ten thousand: 18.6 万, 186K), average difficulty, and when the list was found or the direction an expanded group was expanded in. Selection rules gives the three rules, the run's numbers (seed words, expansion directions, found, picked, not picked) and why the others were not picked, with two examples each.
- Curation now returns a reason code for every candidate it leaves out, by number (unrelated, another brand, wrong intent, weaker than those picked, outside a group's direction, or a duplicate of another); each run keeps its not-picked keywords, with removed-by-you and not-reviewed (below the shortlist) filled in by the server. View those not picked lists them by reason, and Add to suggestions puts one in the group the person picks (`nrKeywordSuggest.notPicked`, `nrKeywordSuggest.addItems`). Lists found before keep their totals and show no reasons. A keyword marked as merely close (relevance 1) is no longer picked.
- Edit groups › New group takes a name or direction; Save expands the keywords in that direction into it, the most it may cost and this month's spending shown first.
- Row reasons say why a keyword is worth doing instead of repeating volume and difficulty; the demo answers write them in the project's language, a Chinese direction gets English search words for an English market, and the chat context lists the not-picked keywords with why.
- Wording in all twelve UI languages: dig becomes expand (拓展), picked becomes 选入, not picked 没选.
