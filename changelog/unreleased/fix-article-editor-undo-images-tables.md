## Article review: undo, pasted images and table rows and columns

- ⌘Z, ⇧⌘Z and ⌘Y in the article body take back and redo one change at a time: typing, bold, a link, an accepted AI rewrite, an added image and a table change. Undo no longer goes back to the Agent's version once the saved edits arrive, an autosave never clears what can be undone, an answer from the server no longer overwrites what was typed after it, and Compare with original keeps the history.
- An image pasted from a web page, Feishu, WeChat, Notion or Copy image is copied into the task's images through the server's public download (public addresses only, 20 MiB, PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF, the website's network settings), so the article keeps its own address. One that cannot be copied is taken out with a message to save it and use Add image. Every autosave links the article's images to the task, so they still show after a refresh and on another computer.
- With the cursor in a table, the toolbar's Table menu inserts a row above or below or a column left or right, and deletes the row, the column or the table. A changed table is written back without extra blank lines, and the rest of the article keeps its Markdown as it was.
