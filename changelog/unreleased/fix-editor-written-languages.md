## Article editor: only the languages written, one at a time

- Edit directly no longer opens on empty fields for languages the article does not have yet. It edits the languages the review shows, one at a time in the same language tabs, and opens on the language the review card or the reading page was showing.
- While the primary language waits for approval, only the primary is edited, and the languages to follow are named once ("English will be translated once Chinese is approved.") instead of as empty fields.
- Saving with an error in another language opens that language and focuses the field.
