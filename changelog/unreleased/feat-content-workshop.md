## Content workshop: adapt articles and topics into X, LinkedIn and community posts

- A new Content workshop page under Tasks recommends what to adapt in two tabs. Trending now lists the opportunities Opportunities currently shows as trending, with what is rising, its figures and chart, related discussions and the site's related pages; Adapt now opens the form with the opportunity chosen and the most active subreddit ticked. Site articles lists blog articles whose clicks are rising, that were published in the last 30 days or that draw clicks all year, with an 8-week or 12-month chart, AI citations and how clicks moved since the last adaptation. An adapted article returns after 14 days only when its clicks rose or an AI answer started citing it, and a steady one after 90 days. Each recommendation states its figures; nothing is suggested by AI.
- Adapting an article or opportunity again shows the earlier adaptations, takes an optional angle and passes the earlier approved drafts to the agent. Pending trending opportunities offer Adapt into short posts, and the tasks made from them appear under the opportunity's related tasks and link back to it as their source. Every record row now opens its task.
- Pick content chooses any blog article or a new topic (with optional key points and links), then the forms to make: X posts (3 single posts, the link in the first reply), a LinkedIn post (within 1,300 characters) and community posts, one task per place (the project's own community sections, subreddits with recent related discussions or entered by hand, and Hacker News). Xiaohongshu notes are shown as coming soon.
- Each form has a built-in playbook in the new Content repurposing category with a Skill of its own: the agent adapts, you review, and approving completes the task. The review card shows each piece with a copy button, its length against the platform's limit and the source sections it comes from, NocoReach's own checks, and for a community post why it goes there and the place's rules. A finished result stays copyable and can be reopened for review.
- A task whose live page is a blog article offers Adapt this article. Workshop tasks name their form and place in the brief, show From Content workshop as their source and the article or topic as their reason, and the records use the task board's statuses. Brand and templates holds the project's own community and shows the voice from Memory.
- The demo's main project adds eight blog articles with their search data and eleven adaptations made from them and from a planned online session; two wait for review. One article's searches rise again after its adaptation, and one steady article was adapted four months ago.
