## Content workshop: adapt articles and topics into X, LinkedIn and community posts

- A new Content workshop page under Tasks shows Trending now, then Library and Records. Trending now lists the opportunities Opportunities currently shows as trending that were not handled in the workshop yet, with what is rising, its figures and chart, related discussions, the site's related pages and any task already handed off from it. Adapt now opens the form with the opportunity chosen and the most active subreddit ticked; Skip takes it off the workshop only. An item also leaves once a post is made from it or after 7 days without being found by the daily analysis. Nothing is suggested by AI.
- Library lists every blog article, newest first, with a filter for those not adapted yet, a search by title or link, the last 30 days' clicks and icons showing which forms were already made from it; hovering an icon lists its tasks.
- Adapting an article or opportunity again names the earlier adaptations, takes an optional angle and passes their drafts, approved or waiting for review, to the agent. Pending trending opportunities offer Adapt into short posts, and the tasks made from them appear under the opportunity's related tasks and link back to it as their source. Every record row now opens its task.
- Adapt on an article or Adapt a new topic (with optional key points and links) leads to the forms to make: X posts (3 single posts, the link in the first reply), a LinkedIn post (within 1,300 characters) and community posts, one task per place (the project's own community sections, subreddits with recent related discussions or entered by hand, and Hacker News). Xiaohongshu notes are shown as coming soon.
- Each form has a built-in playbook in the new Content repurposing category with a Skill of its own: the agent adapts, you review, and approving completes the task. The review card shows each piece with a copy button, its length against the platform's limit and the source sections it comes from, NocoReach's own checks, and for a community post why it goes there and the place's rules. A finished result stays copyable and can be reopened for review.
- A task whose live page is a blog article offers Adapt this article. Workshop tasks name their form and place in the brief, show From Content workshop as their source and the article or topic as their reason, and the records use the task board's statuses. Brand and templates holds the project's own community and shows the voice from Memory.
- The demo's main project adds eight blog articles with their search data and eleven adaptations made from them and from a planned online session; two wait for review, and one article was adapted four months ago. The CRM project's trending opportunity was skipped in the workshop.
