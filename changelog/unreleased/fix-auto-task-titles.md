## Handed-off tasks: titled by their playbook

- A task handed off from a data page, an opportunity or a finished task is titled "<playbook>: <subject>", for example "Keyword research: ai governance framework". The title no longer names an action of its own ("Write content about …"), which could contradict the playbook the task was started with.
- The task keeps its subject. When another playbook is chosen on the start card, the title follows it on the page and is saved when the task starts. The activity records "Title updated to match the playbook". A title a person wrote, in New task, never changes.
- Existing tasks keep their titles.
