## Tasks: Snooze

- A task that waits for something outside NocoReach can be snoozed until a day, as in Gmail: **Snooze…** in the task page's ⋯ menu, above Close task, while the task has not started or waits for a person. The dialog asks what it is waiting for and offers 1 week, 2 weeks (the default), 1 month or a date.
- A snoozed task keeps its progress. It leaves Waiting for me and Current for its own **Snoozed** list on Tasks, which shows what each waits for and the day it comes back, soonest first. The board, By Agent, the menu's task count, the Insights waiting strip and the reminders leave it out. Opportunities and the online agent say it is snoozed, until when and why.
- The task page shows **Snoozed** in the header and a row at the top saying until when, why and who snoozed it, with **Change date** and **Unsnooze**. Its card stays usable: acting on it ends the snooze.
- On the day, at 09:00 in the daily analysis's time zone, the task comes back where it was, and its owner gets one message in the app and by email. The check runs every minute, so a restart misses none.
- The activity records each snooze, a new date, Unsnooze, acting on the task, and coming back. The demo data includes one snoozed task.
