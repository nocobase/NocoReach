## The page beside the chat no longer scrolls into blank space

- With the chat panel open, a long conversation no longer makes the page beside it scroll past its end into empty space (seen on Keywords › AI suggestions while the AI was answering). The screen-reader-only "You" before each question was laid out against the panel instead of the conversation's scroller, so every turn made the whole document taller. The conversation and history scrollers now contain what they scroll, and the panel clips anything else.
