## Tasks: what a task was started with stays on its page

- Once a task starts, the start card's content stays on the left under the card for what to do now, read-only, with the same labels in the same order: what the Agent received (or the document it was handed over from), the playbook with View steps on the steps it started with, the pages or article to change with their languages, where the change goes, and the attachments. It stays through review, publishing, observation, completion and closing.
- Properties no longer lists the playbook, article and repository, and leaves out the related page while the record shows the same page.
- The record keeps the pages the task was started with: relinking the related page afterwards changes Properties and Performance, not the record. Tasks started before this change show their pages as they are now.
