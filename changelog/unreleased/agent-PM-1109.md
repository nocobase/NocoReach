## Inbox: task reminders with details and actions, one Waiting for you per task

- Selecting a task reminder (Waiting for your review, Assigned to you, New comment, Back from snooze) shows the task's status, current step, owner, the output under review and its latest comments, with Review, Snooze… and Mark as read; someone who cannot act on the task is told why.
- A task keeps one Waiting for you reminder: the earlier one is removed when the task waits for review again, and once the task is reviewed, cancelled or archived the reminder reads as Handled.
- Other NocoReach messages, such as a failed daily analysis, show their whole text in the detail pane.
