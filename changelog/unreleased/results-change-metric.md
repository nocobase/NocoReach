## Results: each change judged by the first metric with enough data

- A change is judged by search clicks when there are at least 40 in the days before and after going live together, otherwise by search impressions (at least 200), otherwise by views (at least 40). A change under 15%, or under 10 clicks, 50 impressions or 10 views, is no clear change. Before, only clicks or else views counted, so pages with few views but plenty of impressions were all “Not enough data”.
- Results › Changes names the metric each line was judged by ("Down · Search impressions 6,071 → 4,318 · ↓ 29% · day 7"), draws green for up, red for down and grey for no clear change, and draws no bar when nothing had enough data, giving every total there was instead ("Not enough data: Search clicks 19, Search impressions 35, Views 6 in the 7 days before and after going live, too few to judge"). Its ⓘ gives the rule.
- A result is written the same wherever it appears: metrics are always Search clicks, Search impressions or Views, and changes always read "↑ 208%" or "↓ 29%". The task page's Done card reads "Day 7: Up · Search impressions 170 → 524 · ↑ 208%", the status chip on the board and the list "Day 7: Search impressions ↑ 208%", Activity "recorded the day 7 result: Search impressions ↑ 208%", and Performance gives the same reason as Results when there is not enough data. The Agent's and daily analysis's task summaries use the Done card's wording.
- Day 7 and day 28 results already recorded are judged again once under the new rule, from their stored numbers: they keep their dates and add no line to Activity.
- In the demo, the workload page (Acme) and the renewals page (CRM) stay “Not enough data” under the new rule: their long-tail searches no longer draw more impressions than the page's own keywords.
