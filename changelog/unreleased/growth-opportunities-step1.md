## Opportunities: growth opportunities in four categories, with their growth potential and evidence

- Opportunities lists growth opportunities only, each with a growth the server computed, in four categories: Trending now (what people discuss more lately: rising searches, new searches, a topic heating up, a new GitHub project), New content (what people search for and the site does not cover yet), Competitor gaps (competitor pages, AI recommending competitors, roundups naming only competitors, competitor launches) and Content optimization (what existing pages can still win or win back).
- A searched keyword group without a page counts as Trending now when it has 12 months of search volume and its last 3 months clearly rose (Analysis rules › Searched keywords we do not rank for › Rise over the last 3 months for Trending now); otherwise it is New content.
- Content optimization brings back the drops that cost traffic, each with what it can win back: tracked keywords a page fell for (with each keyword's positions, its click change and the sites now above it), a page's falling search clicks, outdated and thin pages with visits, AI answers that stopped naming us (from the AI search brand lookup two weeks or more before) and site audit issues on pages with search clicks. Each has its least gain or traffic in Analysis rules; a page that fell in the rankings is not offered again for its falling clicks.
- Unanswered forum topics and Discord or Slack questions, pages with many visits and few conversions, releases, stars, downloads, mentions and recurring community topics stay in Insights under Current situation.
- Each opportunity shows its category and kind as two tags, a Growth potential row of up to four figures, and an Evidence table of up to ten rows with View all to the page that lists all of them. Layout, Hand off to agent, Ignore and numbering are unchanged.
- A category filter under the Pending, Watching and Handled tabs counts each category and is kept in the address (`?category=trending`). Pending opportunities are ordered by priority, then Trending now, then the newest.
- Where to change says whether the change is on a new page, an existing page or off the site.
- Insights' daily analysis counts the waiting opportunities by category under its headline (each opening Opportunities on it) instead of the two numbers that moved most, shows its first note under Most worth a look today with the opportunity it is about, folds the rest, and starts each waiting line with its category tag.
- Seven new analysis rules find growth points in data already collected, with no new paid queries: Pages found by new searches, Searched keywords we do not rank for, Keywords with rising searches, Competitor pages ranking where we do not, AI answers naming others, not us, Third-party pages AI cites often, and New popular projects in followed topics. Each has its thresholds in Analysis rules.
- Pages close to page one and pages with many impressions but few clicks now estimate the extra clicks a month from a published click-through rate by position (Backlinko), as impressions × (target rate − current rate), and become opportunities only above a minimum gain that Analysis rules sets.
- The server computes every figure and evidence row; the model only words the opportunity and can no longer change the numbers.
- Estimates are rounded to two significant figures wherever they appear (1,100 extra visits, not 1,073); measured figures such as search volume, impressions and stars stay exact.
- An opportunity that writes a new page says so under Where to change, with the section it would go under and the existing pages to link to it from, instead of an address that does not exist yet; the task brief carries the same lines.
- Growth potential never cuts a figure short: a long name, a unit or a breakdown wraps to a second line, and on a phone the detail stays within the screen with the evidence table scrolling sideways.
- The daily analysis prompt asks for the product feature or page each opportunity serves, titles that say how much can be won back, and notes in order: the finding most worth seeing (Trending now preferred), the other opportunities, then drops and risks.
- The demo data has at least two growth opportunities of each category for ACME and every kind across ACME and ACME CRM, with figures that match their evidence.
