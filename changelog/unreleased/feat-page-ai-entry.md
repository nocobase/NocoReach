## Analysis rules: the AI is in the chat panel

- AI team › Analysis rules no longer has its own AI box. Let AI adjust the rules, beside the refresh button, opens the chat panel on a new conversation that has already read the scheme in view, with four prompts to click: Flag fewer small fluctuations, Add a rule for competitors releasing new versions, Explain why rules were triggered in the latest analysis, Which rules have not been triggered lately?. Opening it again returns to that conversation; earlier ones are in the chat's history.
- In the chat the AI reads the rules (`analysis_rules`: each rule's numbers and range, how many changes it found last time and the latest ones) and proposes one change at a time (`propose_rule_changes`): switches and numbers, a new rule in words, or a changed custom rule. The proposal saves nothing. It is drawn in the conversation with each change's old and new value and its reason, and Confirm changes saves it through the page's own action against the rules as they were read; the rules page updates at once. A proposal the rules moved past reads Out of date. Only people who may change the rules get a proposal or a Confirm button. The same card shows in Insights' conversation.
- The Add rule dialog is the rule's form alone: describing a rule for the AI to draft happens in the chat.
- `PageAiEntry` and `RowAiEntry` (`client/online-agent/page-entry.tsx`) are the one way a page opens the chat with its context and prompts (a page subject on the conversation; the server adds the page's data in `PAGE_CONTEXTS`). Keywords is next.
- Copy in all ten UI languages (`analysisRules.aiEntry*`, `analysisRules.preset*`, `analysisRules.proposal*`, `onlineAgent.readRules`, `onlineAgent.proposeRules`, `onlineAgent.suggestions`); the old box's copy is removed.
