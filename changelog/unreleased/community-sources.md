## YouTube: read through DataForSEO instead of a collector

- Data sources › YouTube reads YouTube through the project's DataForSEO connection, like Reddit discussions: no collector, address or token. Each of up to 5 search terms is searched once a day (about $0.002 a search, first page, by relevance), and each newly found video is read once more for likes and comments (about $0.006 a video). Videos tracked by link (up to 5) are read on every read. Daily reading is on by default; Read now reads at once.
- Spending counts against the DataForSEO monthly budget. The drawer shows the estimated monthly cost and this month's spend, also while daily reading is off ("turned on, it would cost about …"); no per-read cost is shown. When the budget runs out, YouTube pauses until the 1st of next month or until the budget is raised, and says the day ("resumes on Dec 1"): Read now is disabled with that reason on hover, the Update now button on Community › YouTube is disabled with the same line, and the Data health row reads "raise the budget, or reading resumes on Dec 1" with no Retry or Change settings. Raising the budget lifts the pause at once.
- A search term that times out or fails leaves the other terms' results in place. A single miss is a grey note in the drawer and in Data health ("not read this time: …; read again tomorrow") and YouTube stays connected; only a term missed on 3 daily reads in a row needs attention (`missedRuns` in the saved state, cleared when the term is read or the terms change; Read now adds no day). "Tool version" wording and the collector error banner on Community › YouTube are gone; its source line reads the terms, daily reading and the update time.
- Community › YouTube, Bilibili and Xiaohongshu: rows no longer repeat read times ("Collected …", "Metrics read at …"); the source line says "Updated …" once, and its ⓘ says the counts are those of each item's latest read. YouTube rows show the channel only.
- Shortened counts in Chinese keep the unit with the word after it ("1.6 万次播放", also for Xiaohongshu likes and the keyword volume total); English stays "16.2K views". Sentences joined after Chinese or Japanese text no longer get a stray space.
- "Recording since" counts from the earliest saved video, including those the collector saved. Demo and mock videos have realistic titles without a product-name prefix, and the demo spreads their first-saved times over the past four weeks, never before publication.
- YouTube's "sort by upload date" is not sent: YouTube stopped honouring it in January 2026, so searches are by relevance.
- Existing search terms, tracked videos and saved videos carry over; saved videos merge by video ID and keep captions read earlier. Daily reading turns on for configured projects. The YouTube collector path, its yt-dlp provider and the YouTube collector connection are removed; the proxy switch for YouTube is DataForSEO's.
