## Backlinks: broken pages to win links back, and a monthly check that feeds the daily analysis

- Link profile has a Broken pages tab: the site's pages that no longer open (4xx or 5xx) while other sites still link to them, most linked first, with their status code and link counts. The Broken backlinks tile opens it. On your own site, selected pages go to the agent as one Technical fix task that redirects each address to the closest live page or restores it, so its links count again. The online agent can read and propose this lookup too.
- Link profile has a switch, off by default, to check our own site's backlinks every month (about $0.08 a month from the DataForSEO budget): the overview and broken pages are looked up again once the saved ones are 30 days old. Switching it on runs the first check at once; a check that fails (the month's budget, a refusal) says why under the switch and is tried again a day later, not every hour.
- The daily analysis reads those saved lookups through two new analysis rules on the Backlinks source: the latest month losing referring domains on balance or backlinks clearly down (`backlinks-lost`), and our broken pages that still have links (`backlinks-broken`, the five most linked, each a Technical fix). Their numbers can be changed on Analysis rules, where the source reads disconnected while the check is off, waiting until its first lookup, in error after a failed check, and ready afterwards. Only whole months count toward a loss, and the analysis reads these lookups only while the check is on.
