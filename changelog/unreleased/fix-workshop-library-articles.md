## Content workshop: Library lists articles only, by the day they were published

- Library no longer lists pages that list articles: a blog or changelog's later pages (such as /changelog/page/3/), and its tag, category, author and archive pages.
- Articles are ordered by the day they say they were published: Website content now reads it from each page (its publish date markup, else the date written under the title), and reads every page once more to fill it in. Before, a site whose sitemap has no dates showed the day NocoReach first saw the page, so new listing pages came first.
